# Affiliate Compliance Requirements

**Status:** Phase 2 (affiliates are not onboarded in v1). This is a short requirements draft meant to
become an exhibit to a full Affiliate Agreement, which counsel should draft or review separately.

These requirements apply to any third party ("**Affiliate**") that sends leads to
**{{COMPANY_LEGAL_NAME}}** ({{BRAND_NAME}}) ("**Company**"), whether by API, file or any other method.

## 1. Consent language

1.1 Affiliate must display Company's **current approved consent disclosure, verbatim**, in the
language of the page (English or Spanish), for the correct insurance line. Company provides the text
and its consent version ID. No edits, abbreviations, translations or added names.

1.2 The disclosure must be clear and conspicuous: next to the submit button, the same size as the
form's body text, high contrast, not hidden in a scroll box, pop-up, or link.

1.3 The button label named in the disclosure must match the actual button exactly.

1.4 Every lead must include the consent version ID it was captured under. Leads with a missing,
retired or wrong version ID will be rejected.

## 2. Consent proof

2.1 Every lead must include a valid **TrustedForm certificate URL** for the session in which the
consumer gave consent. Company will claim/verify certificates. Leads without a valid certificate, or
whose certificate does not show the approved disclosure, will be rejected and not paid.

2.2 Affiliate must also keep its own consent records (page, timestamp, IP, user agent, disclosure
text) for at least **five (5) years** **[COUNSEL: confirm period]** and provide them within
**two (2) business days** of request.

## 3. Prohibited practices (unless Company approves in writing in advance)

- Incentivized leads (rewards, gift cards, payments, or prizes for submitting a form)
- Sweepstakes, contests or giveaways
- Co-registration or "path" offers where the insurance consent is bundled with other offers
- Lead re-use, recycling, buying leads from other sources, or selling the same lead to others as exclusive
- Pre-checked consent boxes or consent hidden in terms
- Fake, bot-generated, or "pre-filled" submissions; submitting data on behalf of a consumer
- Calling, texting or emailing the consumer to "warm up" the lead, unless approved
- Any creative that is false or misleading about price, benefits, or "free" coverage

## 4. No government look-alike creative

Affiliate must not use any ad, page, mailer or message that looks like it comes from, or is approved
by, the government. This includes Medicare, Social Security, CMS, the U.S. flag or eagle styled as an
official seal, "official notice," "final notice," "benefits update," or similar wording, or logos,
card images, colors or layouts that imitate government documents. **[COUNSEL: confirm applicable
rules, e.g., 42 U.S.C. § 1320b-10 and 39 U.S.C. § 3001, and any state rules.]**

All creative, landing pages and traffic sources must be submitted to Company for approval **before**
use. Company keeps an approval record for each item.

## 5. Traffic and geography

Affiliate may send only leads from consumers in the states, lines, languages and age ranges Company
has approved, and only from traffic sources listed in its approved insertion order.

## 6. Audit rights

Company may, at any time and without advance notice: review any page, ad, mailer, script or traffic
source; request consent records, certificates, and source/sub-ID data; and use test submissions or
"secret shopper" checks. Affiliate must cooperate fully and promptly.

## 7. Suppression and opt-outs

Affiliate must honor opt-outs it receives, forward them to Company within **one (1) business day**,
and scrub against Company's suppression list when Company provides it.

## 8. Quality and payment

Company may reject or reverse payment for leads that fail validation, lack valid consent, are
duplicates, or are later returned by buyers for allowed reasons. Payouts are a flat amount per
accepted lead.

## 9. Immediate termination

Company may suspend or terminate an Affiliate **immediately, without notice**, and withhold payment
for affected leads, for any violation of these requirements, any consumer complaint or legal claim
Company reasonably believes is linked to the Affiliate, or any fraud.

## 10. Indemnity and insurance

Affiliate will indemnify Company and its buyers for claims arising from Affiliate's consent capture,
creative, or traffic practices. **[COUNSEL: consider requiring the Affiliate to carry insurance that
covers TCPA claims, if obtainable, and a holdback/reserve of payouts.]**
