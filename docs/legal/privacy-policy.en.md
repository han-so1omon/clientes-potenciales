## 1. Information we collect

**Information you give us**

- Name, phone number, email address (if you give it), ZIP code, date of birth or age
- The kind and amount of coverage you are interested in, and the best time to call you
- Anything you tell us when you call, email or write to us

**Information collected automatically when you use our website**

- IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, and approximate location based on your IP
  address or ZIP code
- Pages you visit, the website or ad that sent you, and campaign codes (for example, from a mailer QR
  code)
- Cookies, pixels and similar tools (see Section 7)

**Consent records.** When you submit our form, we save a record of your agreement: the exact words
you saw, the date and time, your IP address, your browser information, the page address and the
language. We use a service called **TrustedForm** (by ActiveProspect) to create a certificate of your
visit. TrustedForm may **record your session on the form page**, including what you typed and clicked,
so there is proof of what you saw and agreed to. **[COUNSEL: confirm disclosure of session replay is
sufficient under state wiretap / session-replay laws, including Florida's all-party consent statute.]**

**Information from other sources**

- Phone-number checks (for example, whether a number is active and its line type) from service
  providers such as Twilio
- Information from our advertising and marketing partners (for example, if you filled out a form on a
  social media ad)
- Information from licensed agents who contact you (for example, if you tell them you do not want
  more calls)

## 2. How we use your information

- To connect you with licensed insurance agents and agencies who can answer your questions
- To check that information is real and correct, and to prevent fraud
- To keep proof of your consent
- To contact you about your request, by phone, text or email, if you agreed
- To honor your requests to stop contact or delete data
- To run, improve and measure our website, ads and mailers
- To follow the law and protect our rights

We do not use your information to decide whether you can get insurance. Insurance companies and
agents make those decisions.

## 3. How we share and sell your information

**We sell your information to licensed insurance agents, agencies, and marketing partners.** When you
submit our form and agree, we share your contact details, what you asked about, and your consent
record with **up to 3** licensed insurance agents, agencies or marketing partners that serve your
area. They pay us a fee for each request. You can see a list of these companies at
{{WEBSITE_URL}}/en/partners. **[COUNSEL: align with consent disclosure — "marketing partners" may be
removed.]**

If no one buys your request right away, we may offer it again later, including **after 30 days**, to
companies on that list. **[COUNSEL: aged-lead disclosure — confirm.]**

We also share information with:

- **Service providers** who help us run our business (for example, website hosting, phone checks,
  email and text delivery, consent certificates, and payments). They may use your information only to
  provide services to us.
- **Authorities and others** when the law requires it, to respond to legal process, or to protect
  rights, safety and prevent fraud.
- **A buyer of our business**, if our company is sold or merged.

We do not sell information about anyone we know is under 18.
